# Business and executive prose data feasibility

## Outcome

Kubernetes KEP history is rejected for this program's next data phase. It is
well licensed and versioned, but its dominant signal is technical proposal
lifecycle rather than high-quality business or executive writing.

Three substantially better public sources are now pinned in the ignored,
user-only local quarantine. Together they contain 1,293 current Markdown/MDX
documents and approximately 1.258 million words of authentic company writing.
They are promising `style_targets`; they are not yet approved training data.

## Pinned local sources

| PostHog website/handbook | `/contents/`, with a later business-section allowlist | MIT for `/contents/` only | 371 docs; 491,556 words | 650 historical Markdown/MDX paths with at least two revisions | strongest current strategy, management, sales, and marketing target source |
| Sourcegraph handbook | `/content/`, with a later business-section allowlist | Apache-2.0 covering documentation source | 883 docs; 745,919 words | 2,598 historical Markdown/MDX paths with at least two revisions | broad strategy, CEO, communications, finance, sales, and marketing target source |
| Clef handbook | selected values, onboarding, and operations documents | CC0-1.0 | 39 docs; 20,973 words | 40 historical Markdown paths with at least two revisions | small, clean company-culture and operating-principles supplement |

## What the history can and cannot provide

A commit-message screen found 89 single-document changes with an explicit
rewrite, simplify, clarity, wording, copy-edit, grammar, or typo signal. Only
11 were nominally major rewrites. Manual inspection immediately found that:

- one nominal major rewrite was explicitly generated by an automated PostHog
  coding system and must be excluded;
- several rewrites changed strategy, policy, ownership, or process, so the
  earlier text is not a rough draft of the same intended message;
- many remaining changes are mechanical edits too small to teach the target
  transformation; and
- public Git metadata contains contributor names and email addresses that are
  unnecessary for training and must not enter derived records.

Therefore the current history proves that authentic editorial examples exist,
but it does not yet establish a clean task-pair corpus. No candidate revision
pair is currently `training_approved`, and the nominal counts must not be used
as a quality claim.

## Other sources reviewed

- GitLab has a very large, business-relevant handbook and revision history,
  but the handbook is CC BY-SA 4.0. The share-alike and attribution treatment
  for derived datasets or model artifacts needs a deliberate rights decision,
  so it was not downloaded into the initial set.
- EmailSum contains 2,549 human summaries of authentic email threads, but it
  releases summaries and extraction code rather than the underlying Avocado
  emails. Avocado requires organizational and end-user LDC agreements and
  carries substantial privacy and access-control obligations.
- The 37signals employee handbook is stylistically strong, but its public
  repository exposes no clear content license. Public availability alone is
  insufficient, so it remains permission-required.
- The GSA TTS handbook is CC0/public domain and has deep history, but its
  dominant register is government operating policy rather than business or
  executive prose. It is a lower-priority supplement, not a primary source.
- Synthetic business-email collections and Enron-derived corpora were not
  promoted: synthetic data cannot establish authentic task quality, while
  Enron-derived material raises privacy, underlying-rights, and domain-fit
  problems.

## Recommended next gate

Use PostHog, Sourcegraph, and Clef only for an internal, user-only pilot with
two distinct lanes:

1. Build privacy-cleaned, business-section-only `style_targets`, excluding
   people profiles, compensation, legal, security, postmortems, personal
   contact data, private links, and contributor identity metadata.
2. Manually review the editorial-history candidates and retain only genuine
   same-intent prose improvements as provisional `task_pairs`; exclude factual
   policy updates, strategy changes, mechanical fixes, and AI-generated edits.

The cleaned artifacts should remain `training_approved: false` until their
manifest, exclusions, privacy scan, deduplication, and sample review are
presented to the user. This gate does not authorize GitLab, Avocado/EmailSum,
37signals, synthetic examples, or any public redistribution.
